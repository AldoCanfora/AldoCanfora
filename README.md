## About Me
I am a PhD student in **Physics** at the University of Bologna, working on computational models and data analytics for the **urban digital twin** of the city of Bologna. My work sits at the intersection of **complex systems**, **geospatial data analysis** and **high-performance computing**, turning open and sensor data into indicators that a city can actually use.

## 📚 What I work on / Research Interests

- **Urban digital twins** 🏙️  
  Building the data pipelines and models behind the Bologna Digital Twin.

- **LiDAR & urban morphology** 🛰️  
  Extracting buildings, trees and sky view factor from airborne point clouds.

- **Urban building energy modelling** ⚡  
  City-scale EnergyPlus simulations of about 25,000 buildings on HPC.

- **Urban heat & network science** 🌡️  
  Climate-shelter accessibility and heatwave mobility studied with network theory and mobile phone data.
